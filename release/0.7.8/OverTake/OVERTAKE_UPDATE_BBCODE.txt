[CENTER][B][SIZE=6][COLOR=#D8FF3E]BLACKRACK OVERLAY 0.7.8[/COLOR][/SIZE][/B]
[COLOR=#AEB9C4]Lightweight, configurable LMU telemetry overlays — no SimHub required.[/COLOR][/CENTER]

[B][COLOR=#B86BFF]WHAT'S NEW IN 0.7.8[/COLOR][/B]

[B][COLOR=#D8FF3E]DASHBOARD[/COLOR][/B]
[LIST]
[*]Optional [COLOR=#D8FF3E]headlights[/COLOR] and [COLOR=#D8FF3E]wipers[/COLOR] readouts. Headlights show ON/OFF; wipers show OFF, AUTO, SLOW or FAST.
[*]The hybrid battery can be shown as a compact icon with a live SOC fill: [COLOR=#8FE04F]green[/COLOR] while regenerating and [COLOR=#00EAFF]cyan[/COLOR] while deploying power.
[*]Car-electronics readouts appear only when the car publishes those values.
[*]Dashboard field selection is no longer limited to eight fields. The strip grows with its content.
[*]Temporary notices show engine-map, traction-control, ABS and brake-bias changes without resizing the strip.
[*]Pit warnings can illuminate the gear only or the whole Dashboard, and the speed cell stays stable when it reaches three digits.
[/LIST]

[B][COLOR=#D8FF3E]TIMING AND TRACK MAP[/COLOR][/B]
[LIST]
[*]Timing history grows only for complete timed laps, with no empty reserved rows.
[*]In qualifying, Timing and Track Map share sector results: [COLOR=#B86BFF]purple[/COLOR] for the best time in class and [COLOR=#8FE04F]green[/COLOR] for a personal improvement. Yellow flags take priority, invalid sectors clear and other sessions keep the track clean.
[*]Track-map learning and pit information are saved safely; the map estimates the post-stop position from official geometry and calibrated speed until a complete measured passage is learned.
[/LIST]

[B][COLOR=#D8FF3E]TYRES AND DAMAGE[/COLOR][/B]
[LIST]
[*]Tyre compounds come from the rubber mounted on each axle, with the temperature reference matched to that compound.
[*]Tyre bands follow the game's surface-temperature colours while numeric readings stay neutral. The rear-wing warning uses the simulator's detached-body-part state.
[/LIST]

[B][COLOR=#D8FF3E]FORECAST[/COLOR][/B]
[LIST]
[*]The Forecast strip centres itself when only the NOW column remains.
[/LIST]

[B][COLOR=#D8FF3E]FUEL AND STRATEGY[/COLOR][/B]
[LIST]
[*]Fuel offers independent visibility controls for its blocks, rows and columns, preserved in profiles, import/export and OBS.
[*]Visibility controls for Trailing + Pedal, Relative and Tyres now apply correctly and new options start enabled for existing installations.
[*]Planning uses completed-lap references, configurable fuel and energy margins, fractional race progress and correct refuelling during a pit-stop lap.
[*]Fuel and virtual energy plans are calculated in parallel when both resources exist; shared autonomy and pit targets are consistent across Fuel, Dashboard, Timing and Standings.
[*]Pit targets use leader/class finish projections and learned or calibrated pit traversal, with approximations marked when necessary.
[/LIST]

[B][COLOR=#D8FF3E]STANDINGS AND RELATIVE[/COLOR][/B]
[LIST]
[*]Pit-stop count, pit time and pit lap have independent visibility and can appear beside the name, above it or in a dedicated column.
[*]Timed-race headers share the player's real distance and keep leader and final-stop corrections separate from the fuel demand.
[/LIST]

[B][COLOR=#D8FF3E]STINT HISTORY[/COLOR][/B]
[LIST]
[*]Hybrid cars now show starting/ending SOC and accumulated regenerated energy in kWh.
[*]Fuel used on a pit-stop lap is calculated correctly even when fuel was added during that lap.
[/LIST]

[B][COLOR=#D8FF3E]LIFT & COAST[/COLOR][/B]
[LIST]
[*]The panel is compact around its five LED row.
[*]Choose whether the indicator stays mounted or appears only when one of its five lamps is lit.
[*]The setting is saved with profiles and included in configuration export/import.
[/LIST]

[B][COLOR=#D8FF3E]SPECTATOR, SUPPORT AND LOCALIZATION[/COLOR][/B]
[LIST]
[*]Fuel, Stint History and Lift & Coast hide automatically in spectator mode without losing their preferences, and spectator state no longer inherits the local car's garage state.
[*]The control panel adds onboarding and support diagnostics without requiring preset profiles, and Spanish Dashboard copy is expanded.
[/LIST]

[B][COLOR=#D8FF3E]FLAGS, TRACK MAP AND RELIABILITY[/COLOR][/B]
[LIST]
[*]Flags now use LMU's sector-yellow mask and the correct circular track distance; Standings and Track Map also retain their preventive slow-car marker.
[*]Session changes discard stale standings, weather and pit-stop data.
[*]LMU session restarts and slow OBS/browser-source clients are handled more safely.
[*]Track-map learning is saved atomically so an interrupted save does not lose the learned route.
[/LIST]

64-bit Windows 10 or 11, Le Mans Ultimate and its official
[COLOR=#D8FF3E]LMU_SharedMemoryMapPlugin64.dll[/COLOR][COLOR=#AEB9C4]. SimHub is not required.[/COLOR]

[/LIST]

[B][COLOR=#D8FF3E]FUEL Y ESTRATEGIA[/COLOR][/B]

[*]La planificación usa referencias de vueltas completadas, márgenes configurables de combustible y energía, progreso fraccionario y repostajes correctos durante una vuelta con parada.
[*]Cuando existen ambos recursos, combustible y energía se calculan en paralelo; la autonomía y los objetivos de parada son coherentes entre Fuel, Dashboard, Timing y Clasificación.
[*]Los objetivos de parada usan la llegada estimada del líder y de la clase, además del recorrido por boxes aprendido o calibrado, marcado como aproximado cuando corresponde.
